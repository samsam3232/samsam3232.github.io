Our work with [Abeer Assy](https://www.sentence-processing-lab.sites.tau.ac.il/), an adjacent product of my garden-path research, was presented at the 38th Annual Conference on Human Sentence Processing in College Park, Maryland.  
We ran two large-scale single-trial experiments (n=4,560) showing that plausibility and transitivity bias both shape partial reanalysis in garden-path sentences.
